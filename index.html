How do I start using DumasWriter?＋

Install the Mac app through the App Store, then open DumasWriter on your Mac. This website introduces the app and its features; your writing happens in the installed app. Follow the getting-started guide →

Where is my writing saved?＋

In the Library folder you choose on your Mac. Books are folders and chapters are plain Markdown files, saved automatically as you write. This website does not collect or store your manuscripts. If you choose a folder managed by a cloud or backup service, that service controls any syncing.

Can I write offline?＋

Yes. Once DumasWriter is installed and your writing files are available on your Mac, you can write without an internet connection. Scenes and sound are part of the app. You’ll need an internet connection to install it from the App Store.

How do I export my book?＋

In the app, choose File → Export Writing. Select your chapter, paper, or whole book, then choose PDF, Word, Markdown, or EPUB. Your working files stay in your Library. See the export guide →

How do I keep a backup?＋

Use Writing → Backups and Recovery to make a backup of your Library. You can also enable daily backups while the app is open. Draft History and Recovery helps you revisit earlier versions; a separate backup is still useful for protecting your work.

Which devices can I use?＋

DumasWriter is a native Mac app. Check its Mac App Store listing for the current system requirements, price, and availability.
